Gatherings & Festivals

The US Ocarina Festival (Allen, TX)

An annual event held in Allen, Texas. Started in 2014 as a small gathering of online players, it has grown into an international event featuring performers from Norway, China, Japan, and across the United States.

  • History: Concert videos from 2014 and 2015 are archived online through the Allen, TX television station.

  • Details: Check the official Facebook Event Page for upcoming schedules and guest announcements.

Full Ocarina Buying Guide

Buyer Beware: Only buy ocarinas—especially Zelda replicas—from trusted retailers. Excessively low prices typically indicate a poorly tuned knockoff. If you are unsure about an instrument, send a message to verify quality before purchasing.

Zelda Replicas

Many Zelda replicas feature a heavily rounded shape that creates awkward finger positioning for beginners. The following models balance aesthetics with proper physical playability:

  • STL Zelda Double: Best overall double-chamber pick for playability without compromising ergonomics.

  • STL Zelda Replica ($50): Best entry-level single-chamber replica. Good tone, though the rounded body slightly impacts physical ergonomics to match the game design.

  • Rotter Zelda Replica / 6th Street Ocarina: Alternate physical shapes that deviate slightly from the in-game design to offer improved ergonomics. Rotter is recommended for European buyers.

  • Spencer Register Zelda Replica: Handcrafted, premium-grade instruments. Highly customizable for experienced players, though significantly more expensive.

  • Avoid: Novelty ocarinas using "in-game" fingering systems instead of standard 12-hole layouts.

Plastic Ocarinas

  • Alto / Tenor C:

    • STL New Plastic AC (available with or without Triforce logo)

    • Osawa Focalink AC (requires less breath effort than the standard version)

    • Focalink, Zin, Zin for Children (keyed), Noble, and Condor

  • Soprano C:

    • STL: Highest overall volume.

    • Osawa-style Focalink: Softest overall volume with a mellow tone.

    • Zin: Balanced volume and tone profile.

  • Bass C:

    • STL: Affordable and easy to source.

    • Zin: Larger body, quieter tone, slightly higher price point.

  • Multi-Chambered Plastic:

    • STL Zelda Plastic Double ($40): Low-cost double-chamber option; handles moisture buildup well.

    • Solist Triple / STL/TNG Triples: Entry-level practice options (Solist preferred).

Wooden Ocarinas

  • North Country Workshop: Specializes in double-chambered polyphonic wooden ocarinas using the English pendant system. Requires specialized instruction (lessons available via Cris, Cornell Kinderknecht, or Nancy Rumbel).

  • Hind Ocarina: Transverse models feature a delicate tone requiring a very light, even breath stream. Altos/tenors offer a warm, breathy texture. Obelisk models are recommended, though thumb-hole placement is reversed.(Note: Inline models are not recommended due to an exceptionally light breath requirement).

  • Woodsound: Made by Hyunil Ko. Excellent craftsmanship and build quality. Custom tuning is available. Note that double-ocarina ergonomics can be tight depending on hand size.

Trusted Vendors & Makers

  • Oberon Ocarinas: Top-tier US-made ceramic ocarinas. Transverse models are limited in availability and pricier,but feature exceptional tuning and build quality.

  • STL Ocarina: stlocarina.com – Manufacturer of the Aria design. Reliable catalog for beginners through advanced players.

  • Songbird Ocarina: songbirdocarina.com – US distributor for Focalink, featuring unique original glaze finishes and designs.

  • Takashi Ocarinas: pankuolong.url.tw – Handcrafted by Kuolong Pan in Taiwan. Exceptional master-grade tuning with custom options.

  • Night Ocarina: Maker of the Night by Noble line. Premium Fioretto ceramic series offers professional-grade precision.

  • Focalink: Led by Zack Shih. Known for strict manufacturing consistency, clean tuning, and reliable customer service.

  • Imperial City Ocarinas (ICO): Handcrafted in China by Song Wei. Highly affordable, rustic aesthetic, and solid modern tuning. Order directly to ensure current-generation models.
